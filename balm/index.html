Shopify theme for beauty and skincare stores

Setting up Balm

Balm is built for stores that sell skincare and beauty. Products carry their key ingredients, routine step and texture, and the theme turns them into morning and night rituals, a skin quiz, an ingredient glossary and a pairing guide, so shoppers know what to use, in what order and with what.

Version 1.0.0 Languages English, Spanish Demo store Balm Get support

Quick start

These steps take a new store from install to a working skincare catalog. Each one links to its full section below.

  1. Add Balm from the Theme Store, then open Online Store › Themes › Customize.
  2. Pick fonts, colors and your logo in Theme settings. See Theme settings.
  3. Create the ingredient metaobject and the product metafields listed in Ingredients and metafields. It takes about fifteen minutes, and it is what makes the ingredient tags, rituals, glossary and "What's inside" work.
  4. Fill in each product: routine step, morning or night, skin types, key ingredients, how to use and the full ingredient list.
  5. Create collections by category, by skin type and by ingredient, and build your menus.
  6. Choose the products of your morning and night rituals in the Ritual section and in Theme settings › Cart.
  7. Create the Routine and Ingredients pages and assign them the page.routine and page.ingredients templates. See Skin quiz and Glossary and pairing guide.

Ingredients and metafields

Balm reads skincare details from metafields in the custom namespace and from an ingredient metaobject. Create them once in Settings › Custom data. Use exactly the namespace, key and type shown; the names can be anything, in any language. Every field is optional: the parts that need it stay hidden when a product has none.

1. The ingredient metaobject

In Settings › Custom data › Metaobjects › Add definition, create a definition with the type ingredient and turn on Storefronts access, so the theme can read it. Add these fields, then add one entry per ingredient in Content › Metaobjects.

Menus and collections

Main menu

Choose the menu in the Header section. Items with two levels open a large menu on computers and a sliding panel on phones.

  • Columns as buttons. Short columns, such as By skin type or Morning / Night, can show as round buttons. Choose all columns except the first, only the last, or none. Links to collections can show their product count.
  • Menu feature. Add this block to the Header to show a product, or a collection such as a kit, inside a large menu. In Menu item, choose its position in the main menu (1 is the first). You can replace the image and write your own label, heading and link.
  • Quick links. Pick a short menu, for example your skin types, to show as buttons under the header on phones and in the mobile menu.

Suggested collections

Automated collections keep themselves up to date. The demo store uses:

  • By category: cleansers, toners, serums, moisturizers, sunscreen and eye care, using the product type.
  • By skin type: dry, oily, combination and sensitive, using tags such as Dry. These feed the Skin type picker and the quick links.
  • By ingredient: one per ingredient, using tags such as Niacinamide, each linked to its ingredient entry with custom.ingredient.
  • Bestsellers and New, using tags.

Home page sections

Add sections with Add section in the theme editor. All of them can also be used on other pages.

Slideshow
Up to six slides with an image, an optional phone image or a video that plays muted, and a heading, text and button. Slides can change on their own, with a pause button.
Skin type picker
Buttons for up to six skin types. Each one shows a short tip and two or three products from its collection.
Ritual
Your morning and night rituals, step by step, with each product's step name. A button adds every available product of the ritual to the bag, with an optional offer text. On a product page, the product is highlighted and its ritual opens first.
Skin quiz
Three questions that build a routine from your products. See Skin quiz.
Shoppable videos
Up to four vertical videos (9:16) uploaded to Shopify. They play muted while on screen, each with a pause button and the product it uses, ready to add to the bag.
Live stream
Your next live with its date, a countdown and "Add to calendar", and the products you will show. See Live streams.
Collection list
Cards for your collections, as images or as ingredient cards with the symbol and benefit from custom.ingredient, and an optional product count.
Before and after
Two photos with a slider that shoppers drag (or move with the arrow keys), next to a heading, text and button.
Featured collection
A product grid of 2 to 12 products, with quick add and an optional routine step on each card.
Real results
Figures and testimonials, with an optional filter by skin type and before and after photos. Use only real figures, and say how they were measured in How it was measured.
Contact form, Apps, Custom Liquid
A contact form, a space for app blocks, and a place for your own Liquid code.

The Announcement bar in the header group can rotate several messages, and shows your next live. The footer has a newsletter block (subscribers are added to Customers with the tag newsletter), your store info and social links, menus and text.

The key ingredient can show as a tag on the main image. A bar with the add to bag button appears at the bottom of the screen once the main button scrolls away; turn it off in the section settings.

Below the main section, the default product template adds:

  • Key ingredients ("What's inside"): each key ingredient with its amount and benefit, linked to its collection, and a link that opens the full ingredient list.
  • Ritual, which highlights this product in its ritual.
  • How it feels: texture and finish scales, scent, how long it lasts and a short description.
  • Product recommendations, twice: complementary products (set in the Search & Discovery app) and related products (chosen by Shopify).

Glossary and pairing guide

Create a page called Ingredients in Online Store › Pages and choose the page.ingredients template. It holds two sections, which you can also add to any other page.

Ingredient glossary

Shows every entry of the ingredient metaobject, A to Z, with its symbol, benefit, what it pairs with, what to avoid it with and when to use it. Shoppers can search, filter by what it does (the concerns field) and jump to a letter. Each card links to the ingredient's collection. Turn on featured on one entry to highlight its card, with a label such as Of the month. A box at the end can link to the pairing guide.

Pairing guide

Shoppers pick two ingredients and see whether they go well together or are better apart, with a short note and the products of each. Add one Pair block for each pair you want to answer (up to 30). The list of ingredients is built from the pairs, so write each ingredient the same way in every pair. Pairs you have not listed show your Ask us text.

Good to know. The guide shows only what you write. Keep it general and suggest a patch test for sensitive skin; the note under the answer is there for that.

Skin quiz

Create a page called Find your routine and choose the page.routine template, or add the Skin quiz section to the home page. Shoppers answer three questions:

  1. How their skin feels. Add a Skin answer block for each skin type, with the moisturizer for it.
  2. What they want to improve. Add a Concern answer block for each concern, with the treatment for it.
  3. How much time they have. A short routine (cleanser, treatment, moisturizer) or a full one that adds toner and sunscreen.

Choose the cleanser, toner and sunscreen in the section settings. The result names the routine, for example Dry skin, focused on dark spots, lists each step with its price and the total, and adds the whole routine to the bag in one click. The result is remembered in the shopper's browser for the next visit; turn that off with Remember the result.

Collections and search

  • Filters come from the free Search & Discovery app. Product type, skin type tags, price and availability work well. Show them as a column on the left or as drop-downs above the products.
  • Ingredient banner. Collections with custom.ingredient show the ingredient's benefit, what it pairs with and when to use it.
  • Quick links under the banner, such as categories or ingredients, with the current collection highlighted.
  • Promo tiles. Add up to four inside the product grid, on the first page, for example to promote a ritual.
  • Choose products per page, three or four columns on computers (shoppers can switch), and one or two on phones. Cards can show the routine step and a quick add button; products with options link to their page instead.
  • Search uses the same cards, filters and sorting. When the search matches a collection, such as an ingredient, that collection shows first as a card. With no results, it shows a menu of links and products from a collection.

Cart and gift cards

  • Cart drawer or page. Set in Theme settings › Cart.
  • Complete your ritual. Choose the morning and night ritual products in order. The cart marks the steps already in the bag and offers the missing ones, with their step names.
  • Free shipping bar. Type the amount, in your store currency, from which shipping is free. The cart shows how much is left. The theme only shows the bar; create the free rate in Settings › Shipping and delivery. Leave it empty to hide it.
  • Empty cart. Show a menu of quick links, such as skin types, and products from a collection.
  • Order note and express checkout buttons on the cart page.
  • Gift cards. Create a gift card product in Products › Gift cards. Customers can send one to a recipient with a message and a delivery date. The gift card page is styled to match the theme.

Live streams

Schedule your next live in Theme settings › Live stream: title, platform (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube or other), link, date (2026-10-15), start time (20:00), duration, and how many days before to announce it. Times use your store's time zone (Settings › General).

  • The announcement bar shows "Next live" a few days before, and switches to "Live" on its own while you stream.
  • The Live stream section shows the date, a countdown and an "Add to calendar" button before the live, and a "Live" tag while it is on. YouTube lives play right in the store; TikTok and Instagram lives open in their app, because they cannot be embedded.

When the live ends, the store goes back to normal by itself. Update the date for the next one.

Journal

The blog page can feature the newest article large, filter by topic (tags) and show excerpts. Articles show the date and reading time, and share links.

To show a product after an article, create a metafield definition for blog posts with the key custom.product and the type Product, then pick the product on each post. Without it, the article shows the product chosen in the section settings.

Motion

Balm moves gently, in a few places. Visitors whose device asks for reduced motion never see animations, and videos and slides do not start on their own for them.

Languages

The theme's own text (buttons, labels, messages) comes in English and Spanish. To sell in another language, add it in Settings › Languages and translate your products, collections, ingredient entries and pages with the free Translate & Adapt app. You can also change any theme text in Online Store › Themes › … › Edit default theme content.

The header and footer can show a language selector and a country/region selector. Turn them on in each section's settings.

FAQ

The glossary is empty, or ingredients don't show on my products.

Check that the metaobject type is exactly ingredient, that Storefronts access is on in its definition, that the field keys match the table in Ingredients and metafields and that each entry has a name. For products, check that custom.key_ingredients is a list of ingredient metaobjects.

Do I have to fill in every metafield?

No. Every field is optional, and the parts that need one stay hidden. Start with custom.key_ingredient, custom.routine_step and custom.key_ingredients: they make the biggest difference.

The ritual shows "Step 1", "Step 2" instead of my step names.

Fill in custom.routine_step on each product, for example Step 1 · Cleanse. Without it, Balm numbers the steps in the order you chose them.

Can I sell makeup, body or hair products?

Yes. Products without skincare metafields work as regular products. You can skip the ritual and quiz sections, or use them for any step-by-step routine.

How do I show a different set of sections for one product?

Create a new product template in the theme editor (Product › Create template), change it, and assign it to that product in the admin.

The free shipping bar says one thing and checkout charges shipping.

The bar is only a reminder. The real rule lives in Settings › Shipping and delivery. Make the amount in the theme match your free shipping rate.

My video doesn't play.

Upload the video file to Shopify (in the section's video setting, or in Content › Files). Videos play muted, and only while they are on screen. On phones in low power mode, the browser may show the cover image until the shopper taps play.

Will updates erase my changes?

Theme updates keep your settings and content. Changes made directly in the code are not carried over, so keep a copy of anything you edit by hand.
